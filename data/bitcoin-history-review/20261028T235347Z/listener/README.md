# Controlled populated-history listener evidence

Every market, Coinbase value and finalized outcome in this subdirectory is a controlled
test response. **Software evidence only; no successful external ingestion, empirical
probabilities, performance or returns.** The adapter's `data_kind=real` and the existing
UI's real-evidence banner refer to its receipt-processing path; neither certifies the
fixture's origin. This label applies to both downloaded reviews/reports and all SQLite,
JSON, raw, model, research and experiment artifacts beneath `workspace/`.

The unmodified actual worker produced both completed results after actual HTTP submissions.
`receipt.json` binds job IDs, fixture provenance and two restarts. First/second downloads
were compared byte-for-byte after restart. The repeated fourth capture adds no event and
does not move the earliest decision, partition or original finalized receipt clocks.
