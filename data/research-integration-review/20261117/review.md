# Separate workflow safety review pass

This is a separate static/dynamic review pass by the implementing agent, not a claim of
approval by another human or agent. Final executed regression receipts are listed in README.

**Leakage.** Traced raw acquisition → availability-clock materialization → scalar fitting →
contract compilation/replay → paper binding → monitoring. Forecasts and labels have separate
roles; source receipts and publication clocks are retained. Labels must be available before
fold-specific training/calibration cutoffs with embargo. Protected outcomes become identity
shells; heldout values cannot select schedules, thresholds, events or trades. Family-wide
boundaries persist across jobs and enlarged windows. Existing regressions mutate protected
outcomes, future clocks and source-protection caches and assert refusal or invariant metrics.
The authentic probe independently confirms late labels refuse fitting. No empirical returns
or selection-adjusted statistical significance are established.

The complete objective-driven test initially exercised a correctly blocked forward bias
decision: its recent prior CLI outcomes were in the protected holdout. It now selects its
independently proposed forecast-revision candidate for positive forward scoring and asserts
actual predictions with no model/source-feature refusals. No protected data is unmasked.

**Reproducibility.** Reviewed content identities, raw receipt inventory/hash verification,
feature definitions, exact dataset/fee/model versions, frozen partitions and portable model
bindings. Reused execution IDs now come only from locally published datasets; snapshot hashes
still pass through the existing verified normalization. The new integration uses actual
acquisition/materialization/fitting/replay and restart/restore, with explicit synthetic
selection only at provider/source/evidence boundaries. A global publisher monkeypatch that
could leak through a later module import was removed; the controlled acquisition seam is
scoped to ResearchService. Authentic downloaded datasets were reverified against original
bytes and unchanged persisted experiments after the runner process exited and after restore.

**Permissions.** Source discovery evaluates fixed reviewed catalog IDs. Proposed URLs cannot
authorize fetching. The transport accepts allowlisted anonymous HTTPS GETs, refuses auth/key
headers and redirects, and enforces request/byte limits. The integration proves zero source
requests before ask-mode approval, one shared download after approval, and no repeated AI
generation after evidence arrival. Scheduled reevaluation cannot approve a pending request
or reset AI/HTTP/byte budgets. Canonical roots/symlinks and status-only collector metadata
remain insufficient. Provider subprocesses inherit only selected research credentials;
loopback local models have no proxy forwarding. No credentials were available/exposed.
Both research writer paths now refuse lease release if their thread has not stopped.
Pre-upgrade evaluated checkpoints receive the same bounded evidence-recheck schedule;
recovery itself neither acquires data nor fits a model.

**Financial safety.** Inspected typed contract authority/strike/rule timing, sized-book
requirements, post-decision receipt gates, exact fee-version selection, settlement accounting,
optimistic/base/conservative replay and stress gates. Current settled metadata cannot become
historical execution evidence. Uncertified/late/conflicting fees remain refusals; certificates
reviewed today cannot be backdated. Paper deployment requires an immutable model/evidence
binding and compatible exact forward features; HTTP callers cannot supply alternate risk or
provenance. Controlled proposals pass only the synthetic service; the production service
rejects all. Monitoring remains pending without sufficient genuine independent observations.
Owner review cannot enable exchange submission; no order method or live activation was used.

The final public shadow regression also caught an early history click being discarded while
other panels initialized. The fix preserves the epoch/readiness guards and keeps the legacy
view inert, with a disabled fieldset, until initialization completes. Native disabled form
semantics also prevent fast keyboard/automation input from silently missing focus. Individual
feature/permission controls retain their own disabled states after the fieldset is enabled.
Native provider verification confirms that the submitted objective is preserved.

**Limits.** Authenticated provider inference, actual private Windows/TLS/reboot/offsite setup,
canonical-data coverage and genuine paper qualification are external gates. Unsupported
market/ensemble/weather-pipeline features are explicit engineering gaps, not evidence waits
that a source download can magically satisfy. No attempt changes statistical requirements,
implements another research system, or operates the canonical collector.
